Rauniyar Group was founded with a commitment to empower local makers and share
regional heritage with the world. Rauniyar Group acts as a vital trade link
between local craftspeople and international markets. Under the leadership of Shivi Kumar Rauniyar,
we streamline the entire export journey—from direct sourcing and rigorous quality control
to global freight and customs clearance—ensuring local producers thrive while foreign buyers
receive authentic, export-grade products.
